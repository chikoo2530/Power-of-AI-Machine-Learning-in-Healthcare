Heart Disease

Cardiovascular Risk
⚠️ Early Warning Signs
  • Chest discomfort
  • Shortness of breath
  • Pain in neck/jaw
  • Extreme fatigue
  • Swelling legs/feet
  • Irregular heartbeat
🛡️ Preventive Measures
  • Maintain BMI < 25
  • Limit saturated fats
  • 30 mins cardio daily
  • Avoid tobacco
  • Manage stress
  • Regular BP checks

Diabetes

Endocrine System
🔍 Common Symptoms
  • Excessive thirst
  • Frequent urination
  • Unexplained weight loss
  • Blurred vision
  • Slow healing wounds
  • Tingling hands/feet
🥗 Prevention & Care
  • Reduce sugar intake
  • High fiber diet
  • Stay hydrated
  • Monitor Vit D
  • Portion control
  • Regular glucose tests
